Settings

Thermals are modeled from sun angle on each slope, cloud cover, and wind. Terrain features, bedding, and movement scores are educated guesses from elevation and weather data, not guarantees. Public land data can lag reality; always check regulations and posted boundaries. Rut dates are rough regional averages. Solunar times are folklore.
Imagery: USGS, Esri. Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen). Public land: USGS PAD-US 4.1. Weather: Open-Meteo. Sun/moon: SunCalc.
